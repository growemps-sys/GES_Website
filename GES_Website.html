Our mission

Growth Empowerment Success equips students, families, and educators with the academic, social-emotional, and mental wellness tools to turn potential into sustained achievement and wellbeing.

Empowerment is a skill. It can be taught, practiced, and measured. That is why our work is built on steady coaching relationships, practical skill-building, and honest accountability, not one-time motivational events.

Who we serve

Students and families

Students from upper elementary through college, and the parents who support them, looking for academic and social-emotional coaching.

Schools and districts

Counseling and student-support offices that want workshops for students or practical SEL and wellness training for staff.

Nonprofits and agencies

Youth-serving programs and community or government initiatives that need contracted programming with outcomes they can report.

Services

One toolkit, delivered in person or virtually, one-on-one or in groups, to fit your budget and setting.

Individual and family coaching

One-on-one and small-group sessions on academic skills, self-regulation, goal-setting, and confidence.

School and organization workshops

Single-session or multi-week SEL workshops for students at schools, after-school programs, and community organizations.

Staff and educator training

Professional development that gives teachers, counselors, and youth workers practical SEL and wellness tools for their own classrooms and programs.

Consulting and contract programming

Custom program design and delivery for schools, districts, and nonprofits, including outcome tracking and reporting.

Featured: educator wellness workshop

A flexible one-day professional development experience for teachers and school staff on burnout prevention, stress management, and social-emotional wellness. It can be paired with lighter follow-up sessions through the year to keep skills growing.

Participants leave able to:

  • Recognize early signs of burnout in themselves and colleagues
  • Manage stress in the moment and across a school year
  • Calm and regulate a classroom or workspace under pressure
  • Use SEL principles in daily interactions with students and each other
  • Build lasting wellness habits and know where to turn for support

Includes breakout sessions by role or grade level.

How we work together

Every engagement, from one student to a whole district, follows the same path and is built around measurable change.

  1. Intake

    A first conversation to understand your goals or scope and make sure we are a good fit.

  2. Agreement

    A clear engagement letter or contract covering scope, schedule, and fees.

  3. Delivery

    Coaching sessions, workshops, or training on the schedule we agreed.

  4. Tracking

    Outcomes measured at the start and at regular points along the way.

  5. Review

    A progress check-in with you, then next steps or renewal.

Meet the founder

Ashley Henderson, founder of Growth Empowerment Success, is a Winston-Salem native and a graduate of Mt. Tabor High School and Johnson C. Smith University, where she earned her degree in Interdisciplinary Studies. She is a proud member of Sigma Gamma Rho Sorority, Incorporated, and is currently pursuing her MBA at Gardner-Webb University.

Ashley serves as an Implementation Specialist with the High Fidelity Wraparound program at UNCG and is also a Community Based Rehabilitative Specialist. A former teacher, care manager extender, and family peer specialist, she has advocated for mental health awareness and family support through Mental Health America and NAMI Cabarrus Rowan Stanly.

As a parent of a child with Level 2 Autism, Ashley brings both professional expertise and lived experience to her work. That experience, along with her years in education and family support, inspired her to start Growth Empowerment Success. She wanted parents, educators, and youth to feel empowered by strengthening the skills they already have and learning how to embrace new ones. She believes growth happens when people are recognized for their strengths and given the tools to build on them.
